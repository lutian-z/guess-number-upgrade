# 猜数字升级版 (Number Guess Upgrade)

经典逻辑推理游戏"猜数字"（Bulls and Cows / Mastermind）的**升级版**。在保留经典 A/B 反馈机制的基础上，引入了动态位数、延迟反馈、位置锁定、时间攻击、提示系统等多元进阶机制，支持高度自由的难度定制，并内置互动演示与每日挑战。

The **Upgrade** version of the classic logic puzzle "Bulls and Cows" (Mastermind). While retaining the classic A/B feedback mechanism, it introduces diverse advanced mechanics like dynamic digits, delayed feedback, position locking, time attack, and a hint system — along with highly customizable difficulty, built-in interactive demos, and daily challenges.

## 🎮 玩法详解 / How to Play

### 1. 基础规则 (Classic Mode)
*   **目标 / Goal**: 破解系统生成的 $X$ 位不重复随机数字。
    *   Crack the random $X$-digit number generated by the system (no repeated digits).
*   **操作 / Action**: 每次提交一个猜测序列。
    *   Submit a guess sequence each turn.
*   **反馈 / Feedback**:
    *   **A (Bull)**: 数字正确且位置正确。 / Right digit, right position.
    *   **B (Cow)**: 数字正确但位置错误。 / Right digit, wrong position.
    *   *示例 / Example*: 答案 `38157`，猜测 `365--` → 反馈 `1A1B`（3是A，5是B）。
*   **胜利条件 / Win Condition**: 通过多次猜测，使所有位置的 A 累计覆盖答案的所有位数。
    *   Accumulate enough 'A's from your guesses to cover all positions of the answer.

### 2. 自由难度设置 (Custom Difficulty)
打破传统 4 位限制，完全自定义：
Break the traditional 4-digit limit, fully customizable:

*   **难度滑块 / Difficulty Slider**: 拖动滑块即可自动匹配答案位数与猜测位数，直观选择难度档位（入门 → 地狱）。
    *   Drag the slider to auto-match answer and guess digits and pick a difficulty tier at a glance (Easy → Hell).
*   **答案位数 ($X$) / Answer Digits**: 2 ~ 10 位任选。 / Choose from 2 to 10 digits.
*   **猜测位数 ($Y$) / Guess Digits**: 1 ~ $X$ 位任选。 / Choose from 1 to $X$ digits.
    *   *非对称博弈 / Asymmetric Gameplay*: 当 $Y < X$ 时，未填写的位置用 `-` 遮蔽，不参与比较。这允许玩家分块破解长数字。
    *   When $Y < X$, unfilled positions are masked with `-` and ignored in comparison. This allows players to crack long numbers in chunks.
*   **智能推荐 / Smart Recommendation**: 系统根据 $X, Y$ 及开启的进阶选项，自动计算并推荐合理的尝试次数。
    *   The system automatically calculates and recommends reasonable attempts based on $X, Y$, and active advanced options.

### 3. 进阶硬核机制 (Advanced Modes)
以下机制可**任意组合**，打造地狱级难度：
These mechanisms can be **freely combined** to create hellish difficulty:

#### 🔀 动态位数 (Dynamic Digits)
*   每轮实际可填写的数字位数不再固定为 $Y$，而是在 $Y-1, Y, Y+1$ 之间随机波动。
*   The number of digits you can fill each round is no longer fixed at $Y$, but randomly fluctuates between $Y-1, Y, Y+1$.
*   考验玩家对信息量波动的适应能力，防止套路化推理。
*   Tests adaptability to information fluctuations and prevents formulaic reasoning.

#### ⏳ 延迟反馈 (Delayed Feedback)
*   **温和模式 / Mild**: 第一轮仅显示命中总数 (A+B)，后续轮次延迟一轮揭晓。
    *   Round 1 shows only total hits (A+B); subsequent rounds reveal results with a one-round delay.
*   **硬核模式 / Hardcore**: 第一轮完全盲猜，第 $N$ 次提交后，在第 $N+1$ 次提交时才揭晓第 $N$ 次的结果。
    *   Round 1 is pure blind guess; result of round $N$ is revealed only when submitting round $N+1$.
*   极大增加记忆负担，要求玩家构建长期的逻辑链条。
*   Greatly increases memory load, requiring players to build long-term logical chains.

#### 👁️ 仅显示 B (B-Only Mode)
*   界面**隐藏 A 的数量**，只显示 B。
*   The interface **hides the count of A**, showing only B.
*   *难点 / Challenge*: 当反馈为 `0B` 时，可能是全错 (`0A0B`)，也可能是全对 (`YA0B`)。玩家必须通过后续变体猜测来排除歧义。
    *   When feedback is `0B`, it could mean all wrong (`0A0B`) or all correct (`YA0B`). Players must rule out ambiguity through subsequent variant guesses.
*   彻底改变推理逻辑，从"定位"转向"集合排除"。
*   Completely changes reasoning logic from "positioning" to "set exclusion".

#### 🔒 位置锁定 (Position Lock)
*   每轮系统随机冻结 1~2 个位置，强制填入遮蔽符 `-`，玩家无法在这些位置填数。
*   Each round, the system randomly freezes 1-2 positions, forcing them to be masked with `-`. Players cannot fill these positions.
*   增加了随机性干扰，迫使玩家调整每轮的猜测策略。
*   Adds random interference, forcing players to adjust their guessing strategy each round.

#### ⏱️ 时间攻击 (Time Attack)
*   每轮限时倒计时（30 / 45 / 60 / 90 / 120 秒可选），超时自动用遮蔽符填满剩余空槽并强制提交，消耗 1 次机会。
*   Each round has a countdown (30 / 45 / 60 / 90 / 120s). On timeout, remaining slots are auto-filled with masks, the guess is force-submitted, and it costs 1 chance.
*   时间越紧，进度条颜色越警示，最后 10 秒数字闪烁，考验快速推理能力。
*   The progress bar warns as time runs low and the last 10 seconds blink — a true test of quick reasoning.

#### 💡 提示系统 (Hint System)
*   消耗配额（可选 1~5 次）随机获得三类提示：某数字是否在答案中、答案（或前 5 位）的奇偶个数、某一位与 5 的大小关系。
*   Spend quotas (1–5) to draw one of three random hints: whether a digit is in the answer, the odd/even counts of the answer (or its first 5 digits), or how a random position compares to 5.
*   提示即时生效，不经过延迟反馈队列，用尽后不可再用。
*   Hints take effect instantly, bypassing the delayed-feedback queue, and are gone once spent.

#### ♾️ 无限模式 (Unlimited Mode)
*   关闭次数限制，专注于纯粹的逻辑推理过程，适合练习算法或享受解谜乐趣。
*   Disable chance limits, focusing on pure logical deduction. Ideal for practicing algorithms or enjoying the puzzle.

### 4. 今日挑战 (Daily Challenge)
*   点击顶部导航"今日挑战"一键进入：所有设置由系统随机生成，答案由当天日期决定。
    *   Hit "Daily" in the top nav to jump in: all settings are randomly generated and the answer is seeded by the date.
